Who Is OSSEN ?

Ossen (ossen.eth) is a 27-year-old collector living in Qatar. In interview he keeps the skull as the public face and talks about the bag more than the biography: high-conviction digital art, especially Argonauts, and a rule that artists make the culture while collectors only decide whether to stand next to it. He has put it plainly — a collector without conviction is just a holder, and burning an ape is a vote, not a stunt for its own sake. The wallet surfaced in September 2026, funded with about 50 ETH, and went straight for rare Argonauts, including a clean 0-trait piece that became the profile and Argonaut #8393 for 40 ETH. He bought BAYC #5085 for 6.649 ETH and sent it to the burn address soon after. Since then the public style has stayed the same: sweeps, artist trades of one Argonaut for one artwork, commissions, and permanent on-chain moves. The name stays behind the skull. The activity does not.
